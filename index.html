01
Under reviewACML 2026

A Layer-wise Study of How Style Rewriting Breaks the Calibration of Zero-Shot LLM Fake-News Detectors

Prabhjot Singh, Praney Goyal, Raksha Sharma

Research summary +

Examines layer-wise predictions with the logit lens to localize confidence failures in zero-shot fake-news detection. Adversarial publisher-style rewrites increased expected calibration error by 0.23–0.35 across nine model–dataset combinations. Manuscript under review at ACML 2026.

02
Under reviewACL ARR · AUGUST 2026

Broken Model or Broken Metric? Rethinking Robustness Evaluation in Fake Detection

Prabhjot Singh*, Praney Goyal*, Raksha Sharma

Research summary +

Uses class-conditional accuracy and AUROC to distinguish threshold shifts from ranking failures in fake-news detection. Tests label-free threshold correction using the benchmarks’ known class balance. Manuscript under review in the August 2026 ACL ARR cycle.

Digging Deeper for Truth: Explaining Why Style Rewriting Defeats Zero-Shot LLM Fake-News Detection

Prabhjot Singh*, Praney Goyal*, Raksha Sharma

Research summary +

Studies mechanisms of style-induced failures in zero-shot LLM fake-news detection. This work is part of the research collaboration with IIT Roorkee; the manuscript is under review at the 20th ACM WSDM.

04
WorkshopICLR 2026 · TRUSTWORTHY AI

LORA users beware: A few spurious tokens can manipulate your finetuned model

Marcel Mateos Salles*, Praney Goyal*, Pradyut Sekhsaria, Hai Huang, Randall Balestriero

Research summary +

Investigates Seamless Spurious Token Injection: a vulnerability where very few correlated tokens can dominate predictions in LoRA-fine-tuned LLMs. Experiments across model families and datasets reveal a tradeoff between resource-efficient adaptation and robustness, and limitations of existing data sanitization methods.

arXiv preprint ↗OpenReview ↗
05
Workshop · PosterNEURIPS 2025 · LLM LIFECYCLE

Paraphrasing Away Malicious Tokens: Improving LLM Finetuning Safety by Filtering Spurious Correlation

Marcel Mateos Salles*, Praney Goyal*, Pradyut Sekhsaria, Hai Huang, Randall Balestriero

Research summary +

Evaluates LLM-based paraphrasing as a defense against spurious-token manipulation across three model families and four datasets. Paraphrasing offers partial protection, exposing limitations in filtering defenses and a broader efficiency–robustness tradeoff in parameter-efficient fine-tuning.
